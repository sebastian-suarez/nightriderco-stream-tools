Add him to OBS
  1. Open Husky Typist.app. Click its paw icon in the menu bar → Enable typing & mouse access. Allow Husky Typist in macOS Input Monitoring for typing. For voice, choose Enable microphone in the paw menu and allow microphone access. The input follows your Mac’s default microphone.
  2. Add a Browser source in OBS. Turn Local file off and enter http://127.0.0.1:8876/overlay.html.
  3. Set width and height to 600 × 600, enable Use custom frame rate, and choose 60 FPS. Keep the default transparent custom CSS.
  4. Resize the source to around 260–320 px wide on your 2560 × 1440 canvas. Place it beside the webcam or in a quiet bottom corner.

Pause or quit the helper from its menu-bar icon. Pause stops the microphone, typing and trackpad reactions. The mouth closes when the helper disconnects or you stop talking. If another app occupies port 8876, the helper shows an error.

Tuning: paw menu → Voice sensitivity → High, Balanced, or Low. This reacts to microphone volume, so loud keyboard clicks or music can also trigger it. Use Low in a noisy room. Muting your OBS microphone does not mute this separate listener; use Turn microphone off or Pause mascot.
